Bokpilot

Human-reviewed bookkeeping preparation for Swedish receipts, invoices, bank CSV files, BAS accounts, VAT, and export packets.

Suggestions only. Bokpilot does not replace an accountant and does not provide tax, legal, or professional accounting advice. Export includes approved human-reviewed items only.

      VAT

      VAT is value added tax. Bokpilot calculates draft input or output VAT from gross amounts when the source material suggests a rate.

      BAS accounts

      BAS is Sweden's common chart-of-accounts structure. The app suggests accounts such as 1930 bank, 2641 input VAT, and 3011 sales.

      SIE files

      SIE is a Swedish bookkeeping exchange format. Bokpilot emits strict CP437 SIE 4I bytes; official service validation remains a release gate.

      Human review

      Classification, VAT, deductibility, representation, and asset treatment can depend on facts outside the file. Approval is mandatory before export.
