# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Bokpilot to be a respectful, practical, and welcoming project for small-business owners, accountants, bookkeepers, developers, designers, and people learning Swedish bookkeeping.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and specific.
- Assume good intent while correcting mistakes clearly.
- Treat financial stress, migration background, language level, and business size with respect.
- Keep accounting claims careful and source-backed.
- Protect privacy when discussing sample data or issue reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private financial, identity, or company data without consent.
- Presenting unsafe accounting automation as guaranteed or legally authoritative.
- Pressuring maintainers to add filing, payment, or live posting features without proper safeguards.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who violate this code of conduct. Serious security or privacy issues should be reported through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
