# Product brief

## Project identity

**Name:** Bokpilot  
**Tagline:** Local-first Swedish pre-accounting review packets.  
**Short description:** Bokpilot turns receipts, invoices, and bank CSV rows into explainable BAS/VAT bookkeeping suggestions that a human must review before export.

## Who this is for

- Swedish freelancers and sole proprietors who collect receipts and bank rows before sending material to an accountant.
- Micro-business owners who understand their business but not every BAS account or VAT edge case.
- Bookkeepers and accounting consultants who receive messy source material and want a structured review packet.
- Immigrant entrepreneurs who need plain-language explanations of Swedish bookkeeping concepts.
- Open-source contributors who want a practical accounting-adjacent project without handling live filing or payments.

## Pain points

Skatteverket explains that Swedish businesses must keep orderly bookkeeping, have supporting documents for bookkeeping entries, archive accounting information, and keep private and business finances separate. It also notes that invoices/verifications need fields such as date, customer, business event, specification, and amount, while VAT invoice rules add VAT-specific fields. Source: [Skatteverket, Bokföring - vad kräver lagen?](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/drivaforetag/bokforingochbokslut/bokforingvadkraverlagen.4.18e1b10334ebe8bc80005195.html)

Common pre-accounting friction:

- Receipts, invoice PDFs, email attachments, and bank CSV files live in different places.
- New business owners often know the purchase purpose but not the bookkeeping category.
- VAT rates can be 25%, 12%, 6%, or exempt depending on the good/service and context. Source: [Skatteverket, Momssatser och undantag från moms](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/saljavarorochtjanster/momssatserochundantagfranmoms.4.58d555751259e4d66168000409.html)
- BAS accounts are a shared Swedish accounting language, but the chart is large. BAS provides downloadable 2026 charts. Source: [BAS, Gratis kontoplaner för 2026](https://www.bas.se/kontoplaner/)
- SIE is important for moving accounting data between systems, but correct export requires structure and validation. SIE-Gruppen describes SIE as standard import/export formats for moving accounting data between software and notes that transaction files can be generated by pre-systems. Source: [SIE-Gruppen, Format](https://sie.se/format/)
- Accountants still need human context: business purpose, private use, representation, asset thresholds, cross-border VAT, duplicates, refunds, owner deposits, and missing documentation.

## Existing tools and gap

Commercial Swedish systems already cover real bookkeeping, invoicing, payments, and integrations:

- Fortnox exposes accounting APIs and resources such as supplier invoices and vouchers, but using them requires a real connected environment and careful permissions. Source: [Fortnox API docs](https://api.fortnox.se/apidocs)
- Visma eAccounting offers an API for invoices, expenses, customers, and financial reports in Sweden using OAuth/OpenID access. Source: [Visma Developer eAccounting API](https://developer.visma.com/api/eaccounting)
- Bokio can export SIE4, verifications, receipts, customers, invoices, and other data from active plans. Source: [Bokio export documentation](https://www.bokio.se/hjalp/bokforing/exportera-bokforing/hur-exporterar-jag-bokforing-fran-bokio/)

Open-source accounting tools exist too:

- Frappe Books is a free, open-source desktop accounting app with local data storage. Source: [Frappe Books](https://frappe.io/books)
- ERPNext and similar systems are broader ERP/accounting platforms.

The gap Bokpilot fills is narrower: a local-first, Swedish-focused **pre-accounting assistant** that produces explainable review packets before data enters Fortnox, Visma, Bokio, ERPNext, Frappe Books, or an accountant's workflow. It is intentionally not a ledger-of-record in the MVP.

## Why it matters

Good bookkeeping support should not require every small business owner to become an accountant or every receipt to be uploaded into a closed SaaS. A useful public-interest tool can help people understand the material they hand over, reduce accountant cleanup time, and make Swedish bookkeeping concepts less opaque.

## Why it could become popular

- It solves a real recurring problem before paid accounting tools are even useful.
- It respects privacy by running locally with sample data and deterministic rules.
- It is useful for learning, not only automation.
- It is compatible with existing tools instead of trying to replace them.
- It has clear contribution paths: parsers, BAS mappings, examples, docs, accessibility, and exporters.
- It keeps a strong safety boundary: draft suggestions, human approval, no filing, no payments.

## First version scope

The MVP includes:

- Local web UI and Python backend.
- Sample Swedish receipts, invoices, and bank CSV data.
- CSV and basic text parsing.
- Rule-based BAS/VAT suggestions with confidence and explanations.
- Human approve/reject/edit flow.
- Approved-only JSON, CSV, and SIE-style draft export.
- Documentation, tests, and open-source community files.

## Future versions

Leave these out of the MVP:

- Live Fortnox/Visma/Bokio posting.
- Payment initiation.
- VAT return filing.
- Full OCR/PDF layout extraction.
- Guaranteed SIE certification.
- Autonomous month-end close.
- Formal tax advice.

These belong in later versions with explicit permissions, audit logs, and accountant review.
